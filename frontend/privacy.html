Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Shankari Riverfront Resort respects the privacy of its guests and website visitors. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you browse this website or make a reservation, how the information is used, who it is shared with, and the choices available to you.

1. Introduction

This policy applies to the Shankari Riverfront Resort website and to reservations made through it. By using this website or making a booking, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information provided during booking

When you make or request a reservation we collect your name, email address, mobile number, stay dates, room type, number of rooms and number of guests (adults and children). Additional identity details may be recorded at the property during check-in as required by applicable hospitality regulations.

2.2 Contact information

If you use the enquiry or contact form, we collect the name, email address, phone number and message you submit so that we can reply.

2.3 Payment information

Online payments are processed by our payment gateway, Razorpay. Card numbers, CVV, card PIN and UPI PIN are entered on the gateway's secure checkout and are not collected or stored by this website or by the resort's database. Our system stores only the transaction references needed to manage your booking: the payment order reference, the payment reference, the amount, the payment status and the related timestamps.

2.4 Technical information

Like most websites, our hosting infrastructure automatically records basic technical data such as the IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited and error logs. This information is used for security and to keep the website working correctly.

2.5 Cookies and local storage

The website uses only the storage necessary for its functioning, for example keeping an administrator signed in during a management session. We do not use the website to build advertising profiles of guests.

3. How Information Is Used

  • To check availability, confirm and manage reservations
  • To communicate with you about your stay, cancellation or modification request
  • To process payments and, where approved, refunds
  • To provide customer support and answer enquiries
  • To maintain the security of the website and prevent fraudulent or duplicate bookings
  • To keep records required for accounting and legal purposes

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell guest information. Information is shared only where it is necessary to operate the service:

  • Payment gateway (Razorpay): receives the details required to process a payment or refund and handles them under its own privacy and security terms.
  • Hosting and database providers: the website and booking records are stored on hosted infrastructure, so those providers process the data on our behalf.
  • Communication services: where email or messaging services are used to send booking-related communication.
  • Legal requirements: where disclosure is required by law, by a lawful authority, or to protect the rights, property or safety of guests, staff or the resort.

5. Data Retention

Booking, payment-reference and communication records are retained for as long as needed to service the reservation and to meet accounting, tax and legal record-keeping obligations. Records that are no longer required are deleted or anonymised.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information we hold, including hashed administrator passwords, access-controlled management screens and server-side verification of every payment. No method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely secure, and we do not claim any certification or guarantee beyond the measures actually implemented.

7. Your Rights and Requests

You may contact us to ask what booking information we hold about you, to correct inaccurate details, or to request deletion of information that we are not required to retain. Requests are verified against the contact details registered with the booking before they are actioned.

8. Third-Party Links and Social Media

This website links to external services such as the payment gateway, Google Maps and the resort's social media profiles. Once you follow such a link, the privacy policy of that third party applies; we are not responsible for their content or practices.

9. Children's Privacy

The website is intended for use by adults making a reservation. We do not knowingly collect information directly from children. Where children are included as guests, only the number of children is recorded as part of the booking.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in our services or in applicable requirements. The revised version, with its updated date, will be published on this page.
