About Shankari

Shankari – The Daughter of Lord Shiva

A Riverfront Retreat

Shankari Riverfront Resort is a peaceful riverfront retreat designed for guests looking for comfort, relaxation and memorable celebrations.

The resort offers nine boutique Suite Rooms, a swimming pool and quiet riverfront surroundings. It is suitable for family stays, short getaways and private celebrations such as weddings and parties.
