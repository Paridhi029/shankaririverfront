Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Cancellation & Refund Policy explains how Shankari Riverfront Resort handles cancellation requests, refund eligibility and situations where a stay cannot take place as planned. It applies to reservations made through this website as well as reservations confirmed directly with our reservation team.

Need to cancel or modify your booking?

To request a cancellation or modification, please contact Shankari Riverfront Resort using the registered email address or phone number associated with your booking. Our team will verify your booking and assist you according to the applicable cancellation policy.

Email the Resort Call the Resort

Cancellations are reviewed and approved by the resort team. Bookings cannot be cancelled automatically from the website.

1. Cancellation Requests

All cancellation requests must be submitted through the official resort email address or phone number listed on this website. Submitting a request does not automatically cancel a reservation and does not by itself guarantee a refund. A cancellation is effective only after the resort confirms it in writing.

2. Verification of Your Booking

Before processing a request, the resort may verify the booking details, the registered mobile number, the registered email address, the payment reference and the check-in date. Requests received from contact details that do not match the reservation may take longer to process or may be declined.

3. Cancellation Charges

Applicable cancellation charges depend on the booking conditions.

We request that cancellation intimation be shared at least 48 hours before the check-in date wherever possible, so that the room can be released for other guests. Exact charges applicable to a specific reservation are confirmed by the resort at the time the request is processed.

4. Non-Refundable Bookings

Certain promotional rates, special packages, long-weekend rates and event bookings may be sold on a non-refundable basis. Where a booking is marked non-refundable at the time of confirmation, no refund is payable on cancellation, although the resort may consider a date change at its discretion and subject to availability.

5. No-Show

Guests who do not arrive on the confirmed check-in date without informing the resort in advance may not be eligible for a refund, depending on the terms of the booking.

6. Early Checkout

An early departure does not automatically qualify for a refund of unused nights. Any adjustment in such cases is at the discretion of resort management.

7. Activities and Complimentary Facilities

Outdoor activities, riverfront access, the swimming pool, lawn areas and complimentary facilities may occasionally become unavailable due to weather conditions, water levels, maintenance work, safety considerations, local restrictions or other circumstances outside the resort's control. Such situations do not, by themselves, create a right to a refund of the room tariff unless the affected facility was expressly included as a paid component of the booked package.

8. Refund Processing

Approved refunds are returned through the original payment method. The resort initiates approved refunds after verification; the credit appears in the guest's account according to the timelines followed by the payment gateway, bank, card issuer or UPI provider. We are unable to guarantee an exact credit date.

9. Payment Gateway Charges

Where the payment gateway or banking partner retains transaction or processing charges on a refunded payment, such charges may be deducted from the refundable amount, to the extent permitted by the applicable rate plan and law.

10. Cancellation by the Resort

If the resort is unable to provide the confirmed accommodation for any reason, our team will contact the guest using the registered contact details and offer a suitable resolution. This may include an alternative room category, an alternative date, or a refund of the amount paid for the affected nights, depending on the circumstances.

11. Force Majeure

The resort is not liable for cancellation, interruption or non-performance of a stay caused by events beyond reasonable control, including flood, severe weather, high river levels, natural disaster, epidemic, fire, power or water failure, strike, government restriction, public-safety directions or other similar circumstances. In such situations the resort will make reasonable efforts to offer a date change or an appropriate resolution.

12. Updates to This Policy

Cancellation rules, notice periods and refund conditions may be revised by resort management. The version published on this page at the time of booking applies to that reservation.
